## Capex
- Same tender, same buyer, 4-hour systems, Renqiu Zhihong, Hebei, awarded 15 Sep 2026: sodium-ion 10 MW / 40 MWh at CNY 1.035/Wh (CATL); LFP 90 MW / 360 MWh at CNY 0.518/Wh (Envision; RMB 194.6m over 360 MWh gives 0.541). SMM, 15 and 23 Sep 2026.
- The LFP price is typical: CNESA H1 2026 average 4-hour system bid CNY 0.541/Wh.
- Consistent earlier point: Shanghai Shenneng sodium-ion 5 MW / 20 MWh tender, ceiling CNY 1.1/Wh, March 2025, against LFP near USD 65/kWh then (ESS News).
- Gap: about CNY 0.5/Wh, about R1,200/kWh at R2.3 per yuan; about a third on a 4-hour system at the model's 2026 lithium cost (R13,699/kW, about R3,425/kWh). Caveat: one sodium bid, a 40 MWh pilot, one supplier.

## Outlook (claims, not prices)
- CATL: sodium cell cost to match LFP by end-2026, system cost by 2027 (National Business Daily via CarNewsChina, 9 Jun 2026). The Sep 2026 tender is still about twice LFP.
- Moonwatt: parity in two to three years (Energy-Storage.News, 2026).
- Hithium (6 Oct 2026): LFP keeps an initial capex advantage for two-hour projects; sodium competes on lifetime throughput.
- IRENA technology brief (Nov 2025): makers expect cells near USD 40/kWh at scale, no year.

## Performance (maker figures)
- Cycles: CATL Tener 15,000 (over 10,000 at 45 C); Hithium N162Ah 20,000; Envision 20,000; HiNa 8,000-10,000; BYD over 10,000. LFP comparators: Hithium 10,000-11,000.
- Calendar life: CATL 25-30 years to 70% state of health.
- Cell energy efficiency: HiNa 94%, Hithium N162Ah 94% at 1P, CATL Tener 97%; LFP Hithium 94.5-96%. No system round-trip figure published; Hithium's N785Ah system "24-hour comprehensive efficiency" at least 88%.
- Benchmark Minerals (Nov 2024): long cycle life "not demonstrated commercially yet".
